# Finish: six pages, charts from Manta's recording, cost and connection fixes

## What
- The public site is six pages: Home (the live terminal plus five system numbers), Markets, a Market page, Insights, How it's built and Terminal. The older pages redirect: /screener, /compare, /liquidity, /stats, /events, /quality, /contract, /c, /tracker and /distributions.
- Markets: one page of questions, busiest first, each opening to its outcomes (price, spread, size, volume, gap to fair value, with column help). Category, order and search filters; no paging.
- Market page: built from the terminal's own panels, with an Open in terminal link.
- Charts: continuous (Kalshi skips minutes where the quote did not change; those minutes are drawn flat). Charts lead with Manta's own recording: replayserve `/candles` builds 1-minute candles, and Kalshi's candles fill in the rest. Readout and legend sit above the chart.
- Insights answers what isn't obvious: the cheapest and dearest market, the most mispriced price band (with a caveat), and what arbitrage is worth in dollars. How it's built shows four line drawings with the detailed boxes underneath.
- Header: Log in, Sign up, or Account (no search). Footer: the Manta wordmark on the right.
- Market brief: Claude Haiku 5.5 with thinking off, cached 30 minutes, at most 1,000 calls a day per server.
- Database connections close after 5 idle seconds.
- 546 lines of dead CSS removed.
- Recorder alert: manta-quality emails OPS_EMAIL when a venue stops (10 min) or its coverage drops below 99%, and again when it clears.
- README frozen: the six pages, new screenshots, measured numbers, and what worked and what did not.

## Why
- The finish brief: polish Manta into a portfolio piece Carson can explain end to end. Cut before adding; the terminal is the showcase.
- The brief cost $22 in a day on Opus with thinking; the goal is about $1 a day.
- Insights crashed with "max clients reached" because frozen Vercel functions held all 15 session-pool slots.

## Decisions
- One Markets page of questions rather than a ~1,300-row contract table: a few hundred events fit on one page, and a question is what a visitor looks for.
- The Market page reuses the terminal's panels instead of keeping a second server-rendered book and trades, so there is one version to maintain.
- Recorded candles win over Kalshi's where both exist. They are Manta's own data, and they cover minutes Kalshi leaves out.
- Haiku over a cheaper Opus setup: the brief is a short summary of numbers already on the page.
