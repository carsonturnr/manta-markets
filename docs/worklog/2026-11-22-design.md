# 2026-11-22 — design

## What
- Site look: black page, mint #00ff77 as Manta's colour (its fair values, buttons, links), the market drawn in grey; gaps mint when Manta is above the market, coral red when below.
- Layout: Newsreader serif headlines (next/font), Geist body, Geist Mono labels, dashed rails, square controls, black footer with numbered link columns.
- Header navigation on the public site (no sidebar); public pages moved into the `app/(site)` route group with their own header and footer.
- Home: today's Bitcoin ladder drawn with Manta's fair value at each strike, "Where Manta disagrees most" as dumbbells, what the markets expect, the busiest markets as cards, the waitlist.
- Event pages `/m/[venue]/[event]`: every outcome of one question, the chart, a verdict against fair value, a buy-in-dollars box against the live book.
- Candle chart (lightweight-charts 5.3.0) with timeframes, volume, bid-ask and the fair-value line; Kalshi candlesticks keep their full OHLC, Polymarket history is grouped into candles.
- Markets (screener) slimmed to reader columns; filters apply as they change.
- Pure helpers with tests: `lib/candles.ts`, `lib/outcomes.ts` (verdict, labels, featured outcomes, buying with a dollar budget).

## Why
Carson found the old site hard to read and use: one long document per tool, nine links in the header, numbers without context.

## Decisions
- Look: six directions tried the same day; Carson chose dark with mint.
- Fonts: Geist and Newsreader (Carson's choice); no other new font.
- Charts: lightweight-charts (Apache-2.0, TradingView logo kept as its licence asks) for zoom, pan and crosshair rather than hand-built SVG; the other charts stay SVG.
- Fair value is shown as a second opinion with the gap in points, not as a buy signal: Manta's own record is no more accurate than Kalshi's mid at 5 minutes or 1 day.
