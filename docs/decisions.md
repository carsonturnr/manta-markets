# Decisions

Newest first. One line each: date — decision — why.

- 2026-11-13 — Carson confirmed that Kalshi's permission to display its data publicly (granted for Series) covers the Manta tracker site. — The brief requires this before the tracker goes public.
- 2026-11-13 — Pricing v1 (docs/milestone-2-pricing.md): log-normal probability from the Deribit index, the volatility at the contract's strike from the first Deribit expiry after the close (total variance interpolated when the close falls between expiries), the smile correction −vega·dσ/dK, and the 60-second average as an effective time of τ − 2w/3. Plain Python, no dependencies; data comes from public REST for the demo, and Nautilus's Deribit adapter enters when the pricing runs continuously (Milestone 3). — The simplest sound method the brief asks for, every step explainable.
- 2026-11-13 — First live observation, not acted on: 3 hours out, our distribution is narrower than Kalshi's prices (we're higher below spot and lower above, by 2–6¢). It is either intraday volatility the flat-volatility assumption misses, or Kalshi traders overpricing big moves; Milestone 3 measures which against settled outcomes before changing the model.
- 2026-11-13 — The archive reads crash-damaged files with our own reader (`rawcat` for exports), never `gzip -dc`, and one failing file never stops the night's run. — The first night's archive aborted on the file from the deliberate crash test (gzip rejects a member cut off mid-write) and archived nothing.
- 2026-11-12 — Raw files: a new file per recorder start (`<HH>-<MMSS>.jsonl.gz`), never appended to, and a reader that resumes at the next gzip member after damage. — The first SIGKILL test on the VM found that appending after a crash (Series' behavior) put all later data for the hour behind a torn gzip block that readers stop at. Series never hit it because its restarts were clean. With the fix, a crash loses only the unflushed second.
- 2026-11-12 — The VM keeps 1 day of raw files after archiving (not 3). — The disk had filled to 25 MB free: ClickHouse's internal logs from the stopped Series stack had grown to 18.7 GB. They were emptied (the Series data, 5.25 GB, is kept) and ClickHouse stays stopped. At 6.7 GB/day, 3 days won't fit beside the 5 GB disk guard.
- 2026-11-12 — Recordings archive nightly to a Hetzner Storage Box (BX11, 1 TB, €4/mo, Helsinki) as the recorder's gzip files, copied as-is and verified by checksum; the VM keeps the last day. Carson approved the box. — Measured on the VM: about 3.8 GB/day (Kalshi KXBTCD is about 80% of it), so 1 TB holds about 8 months. *Revised 2026-11-13:* the first version recompressed with zstd -19, which needs about 94 CPU-seconds per minute of Kalshi data and could never keep up; faster levels save only 23–25%, not worth the CPU on the recorder's VM.
- 2026-11-12 — Deribit option quotes are recorded only for options expiring within 2 days with a strike within ±15% of the index, at 1 s; the full surface comes from `markprice.options.btc_usd` (every option's mark and implied volatility, once a second). — Quotes for all 270 near-term options were 2.9 GB/day; hourly Kalshi contracts are priced from near-expiry, near-the-money options.
- 2026-11-12 — Kalshi series are an explicit allow-list (KXBTCD, KXBTC, KXBTC15M), and the recorder refuses to start unless every series' category from Kalshi's API is Crypto, Financials or Economics. — No sports markets ever; Series discovered markets by title regex, which could have matched anything.
- 2026-11-12 — Coinbase and Kraken BTC/USD spot are recorded as a stand-in for the CF Benchmarks BRTI that Kalshi settles on. — The BRTI isn't a free feed; the stand-in gets measured against Kalshi's published settlements.
- 2026-11-12 — Repo `manta-markets`, separate from Series (archived, nothing deleted) and from an unrelated app also named Manta. Recorder: the Go raw tap from Series, extended; NautilusTrader is used as a library from Milestone 2 on. — Carson's choices. The recorder ran 72 h on Series with 0 restarts, and the Nautilus Kalshi adapter is a beta inside a release candidate, too young to trust for data that can't be re-recorded.
- 2026-11-12 — Facts checked against live APIs: KXBTCD and KXBTC hourly, KXBTC15M 15-minute, all `Crypto`, CF Benchmarks settlement, fee_type `quadratic` (taker-only, no maker fee); Deribit dailies at 08:00 UTC; NautilusTrader 2.0.0rc9 is a pre-release (stable 1.231.0). — The brief asked for each to be confirmed before relying on it.
