- 2026-11-22 — Step 12 live relay (recorder/internal/relay). The recorder keeps every recorded Kalshi market's book and latest 20 trades in memory from its own writes (rawlog's OnWrite hook, through the same normalizer as the replay) and pushes each watched market's top 10 levels and trades to the site's server over Server-Sent Events (GET /stream?tickers=…, bearer RELAY_TOKEN), at most every 250 ms per market; off unless -relay-addr is set. SSE rather than WebSockets: data only flows one way, it is plain HTTP that a Next route can pipe through unchanged, browsers reconnect by themselves, and Go's standard library serves it (a WebSocket server needs a dependency). In-process rather than a separate service: the records are already parsed in memory, a second process would have to re-read gzip files that are flushed only every second; the hook never blocks (a full buffer drops and counts records, and Kalshi's sequence numbers then mark the books not-live until the next snapshot), and a relay panic stops the relay, not the recording. A book that may have missed an update is sent with live=false and no levels. Polymarket stays on polling (the recorder polls it every 10 s). — Measured on Friday 2026-11-20 16:00 UTC, the busiest hour on the Storage Box (26.3 million Kalshi records, 7,300 a second, 5,683 markets), streamed through the relay on a development Mac: +2 min 20 s of CPU an hour (3.9% of one core; reading alone 58 s), +100 clients watching 50 markets each +3 s; heap 20 MB after GC, 36 MB peak. On series-1 itself (2 million records of the 2026-11-22 16:00 hour, lowest priority): 6.5 µs a record against 5.3 on the Mac, so about 4.7% of one core at Friday's peak and under 1% on a quiet Sunday; deployed 22:45 UTC, the recorder went from 9.5% to 11.6% of a core and 28 to 59 MB over 5-minute windows before and after (740 records a second). Measuring with rawlog.ReadFile instead overstated the cost (4 min 15 s, mostly garbage collection over the 650 MB compressed hour it holds in memory).

- 2026-11-16 — Step 5 fair values, labelled by method (pricing/fairvalues.py, run each minute in manta-contracts; columns `fair`, `fair_method` on manta.markets).
